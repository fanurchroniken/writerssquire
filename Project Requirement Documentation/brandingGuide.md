# WriterSquire Branding & Style Guide

## Brand Overview

**Brand name**  
WriterSquire

**Tagline**  
Build Worlds. Write Stories. Share Your Vision.

**Mission**  
Empower writers and worldbuilders with professional tools to create, organize, and share their fictional universes while seamlessly transforming ideas into publishable works.

**Core values**  
- Creativity  
- Organization  
- Professionalism  
- Collaboration  
- Inspiration

**Brand personality**  
Professional yet approachable, creative and organized, inspiring and supportive. WriterSquire is the trusted companion for serious writers who value both creativity and structure.

---

## Visual Identity

### Logo

**Concept**  
A stylized quill pen combined with a compass or map element, suggesting both writing and worldbuilding/navigation. The design should convey professionalism, creativity, and organization.

**Primary usage**  
- Full logo (symbol + wordmark) on light backgrounds  
- Symbol-only mark for app icon, favicon, and small sizes  

**Clear space & minimum size**  
- Maintain clear space equal to the height of the "W" in WriterSquire around all sides  
- Do not render smaller than 24px height in digital interfaces  

**Incorrect usage (avoid)**  
- Do not stretch, skew, or rotate  
- Do not change colors outside the brand palette  
- Do not place on low-contrast or busy backgrounds  

---

### Color Palette

**Primary palette**

- **Deep Ink Blue**  
  - Hex: `#1A365D`  
  - Usage: Primary buttons, links, headers, key UI elements  
  - Meaning: Professionalism, depth, trust, the written word  

- **Parchment Cream**  
  - Hex: `#F7F5F3`  
  - Usage: Page backgrounds, cards, neutral UI surfaces  
  - Meaning: Classic writing surface, warmth, clarity, readability  

**Accent palette**

- **Sage Green**  
  - Hex: `#4A7C59`  
  - Usage: Success states, positive actions, growth indicators  
  - Meaning: Growth, creativity, nature (worldbuilding), harmony  

- **Amber Gold**  
  - Hex: `#D4A574`  
  - Usage: Highlights, important callouts, premium features  
  - Meaning: Value, achievement, inspiration, warmth  

**Neutrals**

- **Charcoal Gray**  
  - Hex: `#2D3748`  
  - Usage: Body text, secondary icons, borders, subdued controls  
  - Meaning: Readability, professionalism, clarity  

- **Light Gray**  
  - Hex: `#E2E8F0`  
  - Usage: Dividers, subtle backgrounds, disabled states  
  - Meaning: Subtlety, organization, structure  

**Color usage rules**

- Default primary button: Deep Ink Blue background, white label, hover to slightly darker blue  
- Secondary button: Parchment Cream background, Deep Ink Blue label  
- Success/positive actions: Sage Green accents  
- Premium/highlight features: Amber Gold accents  
- Backgrounds: Prefer Parchment Cream or white with Deep Ink Blue / Charcoal Gray text  
- Error states: Use a muted red variant (e.g., `#C53030`) sparingly

## Typography

**Primary typeface**  
- Inter (Google Font)  
- Use for: Headlines, navigation, buttons, key labels  
- Recommended weights:  
  - 700 – H1, H2  
  - 600 – H3, navigation  
  - 500 – smaller labels, emphasis
  - 400 – default

**Secondary typeface**  
- Source Serif Pro (Google Font)  
- Use for: Body copy in writing editor, long-form content, reading experience  
- Recommended weights:  
  - 400 – default body text  
  - 600 – emphasized body, subheadings  

**Monospace typeface**  
- JetBrains Mono / Fira Code  
- Use for: Code snippets, technical content, timestamps  

**Hierarchy guide**

- H1 (e.g. page title): Inter 32–40px, weight 700, Deep Ink Blue  
- H2 (section title): Inter 24–28px, weight 600–700, Deep Ink Blue  
- H3 (subsection): Inter 18–20px, weight 500–600, Charcoal Gray  
- Body: Source Serif Pro 14–16px, weight 400, Charcoal Gray  
- Caption / helper: Inter 12–13px, weight 400, lighter gray variant  

**Do**

- Maintain generous line-height (140–160%) for readability, especially in writing editor  
- Use sentence case for headings and buttons  
- Use Source Serif Pro in the writing editor for a book-like reading experience  

**Don't**

- Use more than three font families  
- Overuse all-caps except for very short labels  
- Mix serif and sans-serif in UI elements (serif reserved for content)

## Iconography & Illustration

**Icon style**

- Clean, modern line icons with consistent stroke weight  
- Slightly rounded corners for friendliness  
- Primary icon colors: Deep Ink Blue and Sage Green  
- Amber Gold for highlights and premium features  

**Theme**

- Writing and worldbuilding: quill, book, compass, map, globe, timeline, character silhouette, link/connection  
- Professional: document, export, share, collaboration  

**Illustration style**

- Minimalist, professional illustrations  
- Focus on writing tools, worldbuilding elements (maps, globes, timelines)  
- Diverse characters in illustrations  
- Clean, modern aesthetic with subtle gradients  
- Maintain consistent line weight and color treatment

## UI Components

### Buttons

**Primary button**

- Background: Deep Ink Blue  
- Text: White  
- Shape: 8–12px corner radius  
- Hover: Slightly darker blue  
- Focus: Visible outline (2px Sage Green or white shadow)  

**Secondary button**

- Background: Transparent or Parchment Cream  
- Border: 1px Deep Ink Blue  
- Text: Deep Ink Blue  

**Success button**

- Background: Sage Green  
- Text: White  

**Premium/Highlight button**

- Background: Amber Gold  
- Text: White or Deep Ink Blue  

---

### Inputs & Forms

- Background: White or Parchment Cream  
- Border: 1px Light Gray at rest, Deep Ink Blue on focus  
- Corner radius: 6–8px  
- Label: Inter 13px, Charcoal Gray  
- Helper/error text: Inter 12px  
  - Error color: Muted red variant  

---

### Cards & Surfaces

- Background: White or Parchment Cream  
- Border-radius: 8–12px  
- Shadow: Soft, subtle elevation  
- Card header text: Inter, Deep Ink Blue  
- Decorative accents: Sage Green dividers, small icons  

---

### Writing Editor

- Background: White or off-white (`#FEFEFE`)  
- Text: Source Serif Pro, Charcoal Gray  
- Line height: 1.6–1.8 for readability  
- Focus state: Subtle border or background highlight  
- Toolbar: Deep Ink Blue background, white icons

## Tone of Voice

**Overall tone**

- Professional yet approachable  
- Supportive and encouraging  
- Clear and organized  
- Inspiring without being overly flowery  

**Key principles**

- Use "you" to address users directly  
- Be clear and concise in instructions  
- Use professional terminology when appropriate  
- Avoid overly casual language  
- Celebrate user achievements genuinely  

**Examples – in-app**

- Onboarding: "Welcome to WriterSquire. Let's build your first world."  
- Error: "Something went wrong. Please try again or contact support."  
- Success: "World created! Start adding countries, characters, and timelines."  
- Empty state: "No documents yet. Create your first manuscript to get started."  

**Examples – marketing**

- Landing page: "The all-in-one platform for worldbuilding and writing."  
- Feature section: "Organize complex worlds with interconnected elements."  
- CTA: "Start Writing Free" or "Begin Your Journey"

## Social & Marketing

**Visual approach**

- Use the professional color palette in social tiles and ads  
- Combine UI screenshots with illustrated writing/worldbuilding elements  
- Highlight real user creations (with permission)  
- Showcase the writing editor and worldbuilding interface  

**Copy approach**

- Clear, benefit-focused headlines  
  - "Build immersive worlds. Write compelling stories."  
  - "From idea to published book in one platform."  
- Strong CTAs: "Start Writing", "Create Your World", "Export Your Book"  

---

## Accessibility Guidelines

- Maintain sufficient color contrast between text and background (WCAG AA minimum, AA+ preferred)  
- Avoid relying on color alone to indicate state (use icons, labels, and patterns)  
- Provide clear focus states for keyboard navigation  
- Ensure all interactive elements are keyboard accessible  
- Support screen readers with proper ARIA labels  
- Maintain readable font sizes (minimum 12px for body text)
